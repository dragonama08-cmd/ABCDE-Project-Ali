Triage levels

Triage means sorting patients by how quickly they need help.

Red: Immediate

Life-threatening problem with airway, breathing or circulation. Call the emergency number now and start first aid.

Yellow: Urgent

Serious but currently stable. Give first aid, monitor closely, and be ready to call for help if the patient gets worse.

Green: Minor

No threat to life. Basic first aid and routine medical follow-up.

About this project

Task 10: Smart Emergency Triage & Patient Assessment, part of the First Aid & Artificial Intelligence course project. It trains users to assess a patient with ABCDE and recognise cases that need urgent medical help.
